LiftingLog · UI redesign · round 3

Screen mockups

Your round 2 choices are now marked Chosen, and a new section 6 shows the Workouts tab with delete and discard. These screens use the Ink palette with one highlight colour, reserved for things that are happening right now. Secondary buttons now have a darker fill and a border. The date navigator no longer has a Today button. After that come the workout card options, then redesigns of Exercises, the bottom navigation and Settings. Use the controls to switch between light and dark mode and between highlight colours. Every mockup is drawn at real Android dp sizes, then scaled down.

1 · Palette adjustments

Ink, with a live highlight

The highlight colour is used only for in-progress state: the status pill, the live dot and the active-workout bar. Everything else stays slate. Green reads as "running", and it doesn't clash with PB gold. Amber sits too close to gold. Blue brings back the look you wanted to move away from.

2 · Workout cards

Three more compact cards

Each option shows the same day with three workouts. The number under each phone is the height of one card, measured in dp from the rendered mockup.

3 · Exercises

Exercises as a grouped list

The gradient header, icon tiles and per-exercise cards go. Exercises become rows in one surface per muscle group, with the equipment and number of variations on a second line. The pinned-exercises button is removed. An "In this workout" group at the top replaces it. Its heading has a pill that returns you to the active workout.

Search and Add share a rowThis keeps the existing layout: a fixed-width Add button that never moves while you type.
Filters are chips, sort sits beside themAll, Muscle and Equipment keep their current meaning. Sort moves from a separate row into a compact control at the end of the chip row.
Actions move out of the rowThe ⋯ button is removed from each row. Long-press opens the same action sheet as today, and the exercise page keeps its own menu.
4 · Bottom navigation

Three navigation bar options

All three put the active-workout navigator into the navigation itself. With the chosen docked bar, the live strip appears only where nothing else on the screen leads back to the workout. Programs shows a "Soon" badge in the MVP build.

5 · Settings

Settings as grouped lists

The colour-theme picker is removed. Display mode and weight unit become inline segmented controls, so they no longer open a picker. The 1RM formula keeps its sheet, which now shows each formula. Restore is moved to the bottom and shown in red, because it replaces all data.

6 · Workouts, all together

Workouts, delete and discard

This is the Workouts tab with every choice applied. You can delete a workout from two places. Hold a card on the list: it shrinks slightly and a red bar fills along its bottom edge, and when the bar is full the phone vibrates and the confirmation opens. Or use the Delete workout button at the end of the workout's detail page. Both routes open the same confirmation.
